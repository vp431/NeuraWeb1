NeuraCon Brand Kit

The logo, colours, type and tone used on the NeuraCon website. Every file listed here sits in this folder.

Logo

The mark is a tilted ink tile with a bold N and a yellow live dot. Use the full logo (mark plus name) wherever there is room, and the mark alone for icons and avatars.

logo/mark.svg

logo/lockup.svg
logo/lockup-dark.svg
  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the size of the yellow dot.
  • Do not show the mark smaller than 16px, or the full logo smaller than 96px wide.
  • Use the light version on paper or white, and the dark version on ink or photos.
  • Do not recolour the tile, straighten the tilt, move the dot, or add effects such as glows and gradients.

Voice

  • Say what the agents do in plain words: call, qualify, book.
  • Lead with the 60 second promise.
  • Talk about an AI sales team, with agents that each own one job, instead of a platform with modules.
  • Write short sentences. Do not use em dashes.
  • Do not quote statistics without a source, and label sample data as sample data.
