Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

NeuraCon uses the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies

Required for the platform to function. These handle authentication, session management, and security. You cannot opt out of these cookies.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website: which pages are visited, where visitors come from, and how features are used. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings and preferences (such as language or dashboard layout) so you don't need to set them each time you visit.

Marketing Cookies

Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant ads. These are only used if you arrive via one of our advertising campaigns (Facebook, Instagram, or Google).

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use cookies from the following third parties:

  • Google Analytics: For website usage analytics.
  • Facebook Pixel: For measuring ad campaign effectiveness.
  • Google Ads: For conversion tracking.

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing the data they collect.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

Blocking certain cookies may affect your experience and prevent some features from working properly.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
