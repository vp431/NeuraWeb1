About us

The first minute decides the sale. We built a team that never misses it.

NeuraCon is an AI sales team for real estate. We believe every lead deserves a fast answer, and every sales executive deserves to spend the day with buyers who are ready.

Our vision

No real estate lead is ever lost to a slow follow-up.

We see a market where every buyer hears back within a minute, in their own language, at any hour. Nobody waits a day for a call, and no builder pays for a lead that was never contacted.

Our mission

Give every builder and broker an AI sales team that works each lead from ad click to site visit.

Our agents take the repetitive, time-critical work: the first call, the qualification, the follow-up and the booking. Your people do what they are best at, which is building relationships, showing homes and closing deals.

What drives us

Six things we will not compromise on.

01

Speed wins deals

In real estate, the first responder wins. We build everything around reaching a lead before anyone else does.

02

People close, agents assist

AI handles the first conversation and your team closes the deal. We make your people stronger. We do not replace them.

03

Your data, your rules

Every call is recorded and on the record. You decide how your data is used and who on your team can see it.

04

Built for the whole team

We design for everyone in the sale, from the executive standing at the site to the owner tracking return on ad spend.

05

Built only for real estate

We solve for property sales and nothing else: leads that come from ads, buyers who want an answer in their own language, and a sale that starts with a site visit.

06

Better every week

We study what works on real calls and keep improving how the agents listen, ask and follow up.
