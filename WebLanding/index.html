How It Works

From Ad Click to Site Visit in 4 Steps

Set it up once. Let AI handle the rest — all the way to the site visit.

1

Lead Comes In

A buyer clicks your Facebook or Instagram ad and fills the form. NeuraCon captures the lead instantly — no manual imports, no delays.

2

AI Calls & Qualifies

Within minutes, NeuraCon's AI agent calls the lead, asks qualifying questions, gauges budget and timeline, and scores them from 0–100.

3

Site Visit Booked

For hot leads, the AI books a site visit at their preferred time. It sends the property location, directions, and confirmation via WhatsApp.

4

Your Agent Shows Up Prepared

Your on-ground agent receives the visit assignment with full context — lead name, budget, preferences, call recording, and AI-recommended talking points.
