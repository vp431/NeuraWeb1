How it works

One lead, start to site visit.

Set it up once. The agents run every lead through the same five moments.

  1. T + 0s

    Lead comes in

    A buyer fills the form on your Facebook or Instagram ad. NeuraCon captures it instantly, with no imports and no delays.

  2. T + 60s

    The agent calls

    Within 60 seconds the voice agent is on the phone, speaking the buyer's language.

  3. On the call

    Qualified and scored

    It asks about budget, timeline and preferences, then scores the buyer from 0 to 100.

  4. After the call

    Site visit booked

    Hot leads get a visit at their preferred time, with location and directions sent on WhatsApp.

  5. Visit day

    Your executive arrives prepared

    Name, budget, preferences, the call recording and recommended talking points, all in hand.
